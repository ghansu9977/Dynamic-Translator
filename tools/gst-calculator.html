Complete Guide to GST Calculation & Slabs in India

Goods and Services Tax (GST) is an indirect comprehensive consumption tax levied on the supply of goods and services in India. It replaced a web of cascading taxes like VAT, Service Tax, and Central Excise. Whether you are generating invoices, filing returns, or checking retail receipts, calculating GST correctly ensures accounting compliance.

1. Formulas for GST Calculation:

➕ Adding GST (GST Exclusive):

GST Amount = (Base Amount × GST%) / 100
Gross Price = Base Amount + GST Amount

➖ Removing GST (Reverse / GST Inclusive):

Base Amount = Gross Price / (1 + (GST% / 100))
GST Amount = Gross Price - Base Amount

2. Major GST Slabs and Applicable Goods:

  • 0% Slab: Essential grocery staples (milk, eggs, fresh vegetables, unbranded flours), books, newspapers.
  • 5% Slab: Packaged edible items, household spices, life-saving medicines, budget rail/economy flight tickets.
  • 12% Slab: Computers, mobile phones, processed foods, diagnostic kits, business class flight tickets.
  • 18% Slab (Most Common): Software, telecom, financial services, consumer electronics, branded clothing, and restaurants.
  • 28% Slab: Luxury cars, pan masala, betting, cigarettes, and 5-star hotel luxury stays.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. What is the difference between CGST, SGST, and IGST?

When an invoice is issued within the same state (Intra-State), the tax is shared equally between the Centre and the State: 18% GST becomes 9% CGST + 9% SGST. When goods or services cross state borders (Inter-State), 18% Integrated GST (IGST) is charged entirely.

2. How can I remove 18% GST from a total bill of ₹1,180?

Use the "Remove GST" mode on this tool or divide by 1.18: 1180 / 1.18 = ₹1,000 base price, meaning the GST component is ₹180.
