How Cut-off Dates Work in Recruitment and Competitive Examinations

When applying for competitive examinations (such as SSC CGL, CHSL, MTS, UPSC Civil Services, NDA, CDS, IBPS PO, Clerk, SBI, Railway RRB, and Police recruitment), one of the primary qualification criteria is the Age Eligibility Range.

Most candidates calculate their age from today's date; however, examination notifications explicitly stipulate a cut-off anchor date, such as: "Candidates must be between 18 and 27 years of age as of 01-08-2026". If an applicant exceeds the upper age limit or falls short by even a single day on that cut-off date, the online portal automatically disqualifies the application. Knowing your exact age in years, months, and days is therefore critical.

Common Competitive Exam Age Criteria & Cut-off Standards:

Examination Name General Category Age Limit Standard Cut-off Anchor Date
SSC CGL / CHSL 18 to 27 / 30 / 32 years (by post) Usually 1st August (or 1st January)
UPSC Civil Services (IAS/IPS) 21 to 32 years 1st August of Exam Year
IBPS Bank PO / Clerk 20 to 30 years (PO) / 20 to 28 (Clerk) 1st of July or Notification Month
State Police Services 18 to 22 / 25 years 1st July or 1st January

General Age Relaxation Guidelines for Reserved Categories:

Government norms provide upper age relaxation for various reserved categories as summarized below:

  • OBC (Other Backward Classes): 3 years upper age relaxation.
  • SC / ST (Scheduled Castes / Scheduled Tribes): 5 years upper age relaxation.
  • PwD (Persons with Disabilities): 10 to 15 years upper age relaxation based on category.
  • Ex-Servicemen: 3 years relaxation after deduction of active military service duration.
