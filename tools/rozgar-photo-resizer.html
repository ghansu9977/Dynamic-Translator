How to Resize Photos and Signatures for Online Applications

During online job and university entrance submissions, a significant portion of applicant forms encounter validation errors due to incompatible file sizes or incorrect image dimensions. Application portals enforce strict constraints where passport photos must fall between 20KB and 50KB, while signatures must remain under 20KB.

RozgarTools Photo Resizer employs a localized binary-search compression routine. Rather than guessing compression quality, the engine iteratively adjusts the image matrix to match your target file size with maximum possible clarity.

Standard Application Dimension Standards:

Application Type Photo Size Target Signature Size Target Standard Dimensions
Civil Services & Clerical 20 KB to 50 KB 10 KB to 20 KB 3.5 cm × 4.5 cm (350×450 px)
National Recruitment Boards 20 KB to 100 KB 10 KB to 50 KB 350 × 350 px / Square
Banking & Financial Portals 20 KB to 50 KB 10 KB to 20 KB 200 × 230 px
