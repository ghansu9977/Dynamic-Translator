How to Create a Personalized Diwali Greeting Card with Photo & Name Online?

Diwali (दीपावली), the auspicious Festival of Lights, is India's biggest festive celebration of joy, prosperity, and victory of light over darkness. Rather than sending generic forwarded WhatsApp images that everyone ignores, creating a **personalized Happy Diwali wishing card with your own name, family name, or business brand** creates a lasting impression on your friends, relatives, and clients.

Step-by-Step Guide to Generate Your HD Festival Card:

  1. Step 1: Enter Your Name: Type your personal name, family name (e.g., राहुल साहू एवं सपरिवार), or shop/coaching name in the name field.
  2. Step 2: Choose Your Festival Theme: Select from 5 royal color palettes — Royal Maroon Gold, Devotional Midnight Navy, Neon Fireworks & Rangoli, Minimalist Charcoal Gold, or Saffron Dhanteras.
  3. Step 3: Upload Photo (Optional): Add your passport photo or family picture. Our smart HTML5 canvas engine automatically crops and embeds it inside an ornate golden locket with festive beads and glowing aura.
  4. Step 4: Select Heartfelt Hindi or English Wishes: Pick from 6 pre-tested festive shayari and shlokas, or write your own custom greeting.
  5. Step 5: Download & Share on WhatsApp: Click Download HD Card to save a crisp 1080x1080 square image (perfect for WhatsApp DP, Instagram Post, and Facebook), or click Share on WhatsApp to share instantly with 1 tap.
