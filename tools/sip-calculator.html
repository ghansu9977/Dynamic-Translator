Understanding the Power of Compounding via Mutual Fund SIPs

A Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) allows individuals to invest manageable, consistent monthly sums into equity or debt mutual funds. Unlike attempting to time volatile market peaks and troughs, regular investing takes advantage of Rupee Cost Averaging. When markets dip, your fixed monthly allocation buys more mutual fund units; when markets rally, those accumulated units surge in capital value.

The 15 × 15 × 15 Wealth Formula:

In financial literacy, the 15×15×15 principle illustrates the exponential trajectory of compounding: investing ₹15,000 per month for 15 years at an annualized return of 15% yields a portfolio of approximately ₹1,00,00,000 (1 Crore).
