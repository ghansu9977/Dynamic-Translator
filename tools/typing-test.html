How to Improve Your Typing Speed and Accuracy

In modern examinations, judicial clerical tests, administrative secretariats, and technical operations, candidates are routinely required to pass rigorous typing evaluations. Developing high accuracy while maintaining steady cadence ensures error-free productivity.

5 Core Principles of Fast Touch Typing:

  1. Adopt Touch Typing: Place your fingers on the home row keys (A S D F and J K L ;) and keep your gaze focused on the screen rather than looking down at the keyboard.
  2. Focus on Precision First: Speed naturally follows accuracy. Strive for at least 95% accuracy before pushing for rapid words-per-minute milestones.
  3. Minimize Backspace Usage: Frequent corrections break your typing momentum and drastically lower net WPM scores.
  4. Consistent Daily Drills: Dedicate 15 to 20 minutes every day to focused typing sessions rather than irregular multi-hour marathons.
