How to Convert Images (JPG/PNG) into A4 PDF for Online Applications

When filling out competitive exam applications and job registrations, portals frequently require academic and personal proofs (such as 10th and 12th marksheets, degree certificates, category certificates, domicile, and identity cards) to be uploaded in PDF format. Most government application guidelines mandate: "All relevant certificates must be scanned and merged into a single multi-page PDF file under 1MB."

Applicants often capture photos on their mobile phones but struggle to combine them into a single file without paying cyber cafe charges or using third-party apps that imprint ugly watermarks and store sensitive records on external servers. RozgarTools Image to PDF Converter solves this by converting images directly inside your browser without any watermark or server upload.

Best Practices When Preparing PDF Documents:

  • Ensure Crisp Lighting: Avoid camera shadows and excessive flash glare so that registration numbers and grades remain clearly legible.
  • Arrange Proper Chronology: Place documents in chronological sequence (e.g., 10th Marks Sheet, followed by 12th, Degree, and Caste Certificate). Use our ↑ / ↓ controls to sort your pages seamlessly.
  • Use Standard A4 Aspect Ratio: Standard office printers and scanning systems expect A4 layout. Our converter automatically scales your images to fit A4 sheets without distortion.

  3. 100% Client-Side Privacy: Your documents never leave your device. The PDF is assembled using in-browser JavaScript.
