How to Craft an Effective 1-Page Resume

Hiring managers spend an average of six seconds scanning an applicant's resume. Multi-page, convoluted resumes often result in critical details being overlooked. A concise, well-structured single-page resume delivers your credentials with clarity and impact.

Key Recommendations for High ATS Compatibility:

  • Standard Layout Structure: Use universal heading names (Education, Experience, Skills) to allow automated applicant tracking systems to categorize sections without parsing errors.
  • Action-Oriented Verbs: Initiate bullet points with strong verbs (e.g., spearheaded, optimized, engineered, coordinated) to illustrate concrete outcomes.
  • Proofreading and Precision: Eliminate spelling inconsistencies, verify contact emails, and ensure consistent date formats throughout.
