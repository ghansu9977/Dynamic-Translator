Understanding Loan EMI (Equated Monthly Installment) and Amortization

An Equated Monthly Installment (EMI) is a fixed payment amount made by a borrower to a lender at a specified date each calendar month. EMIs apply to both interest and principal each month, ensuring that over a specified number of years, the loan is paid off in full.

During the early stages of the loan repayment tenure, a larger portion of each instalment goes toward servicing the accumulated interest. As the loan matures, the outstanding principal decreases, causing the interest component to decrease and the principal repayment component to rise. This structure is mapped out in an Amortization Schedule.

Benefits of Using an Online EMI Calculator:

  • Budget Certainty: Anticipate your exact monthly liability before signing loan agreements.
  • Interest Cost Visibility: Clearly observe total interest paid on top of borrowed principal.
  • Tenure Optimization: Compare how selecting a 15-year versus 20-year term impacts total financial outlay.
