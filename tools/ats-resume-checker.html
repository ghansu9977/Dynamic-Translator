How Does an ATS (Applicant Tracking System) Read Your Resume?

An Applicant Tracking System (ATS) is automated recruitment software utilized by over 98% of Fortune 500 enterprises, corporate hiring managers, and government examination boards to filter job applicants. Before human recruiters ever review your CV, the ATS parses your document into structured database fields: contact information, work experience titles, education history, and technical keyword density.

Key Factors That Influence Your ATS Resume Score:

  • Standard Section Headings: Always use recognizable headings such as "Professional Summary", "Work Experience", "Education", and "Skills". Avoid non-standard labels like "My Journey" or "What I Do".
  • High-Impact Action Verbs: Start bullet points with strong verbs (e.g., Developed, Spearheaded, Engineered, Optimized, Delivered) instead of passive duties like "Responsible for".
  • Measurable Numbers & Percentages: Quantify your achievements (e.g., "Increased organic traffic by 45%", "Managed a team of 8").
  • Clean Single-Column Layout: Multi-column tables, complex graphics, and embedded text boxes often corrupt automated parsers.
