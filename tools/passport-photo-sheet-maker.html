Official Indian Examination & Passport Specifications:

  • Standard Size: 35mm width x 45mm height (3.5 cm x 4.5 cm).
  • Face Coverage: Face must cover 70% to 80% of the photograph from the crown of the head to the chin.
  • Background: Plain white or light off-white background without shadows or patterns.
  • Expression & Eyes: Neutral facial expression with both eyes clearly open and visible, looking directly into the camera.
  • Name & Date of Photo (DOP): Many central and state recruitment boards (such as SSC, UP Police, Bihar Police) mandate that the candidate's name and the date on which the photograph was captured must be printed at the bottom. Our tool does this automatically!

1. Which paper size is best for printing passport photos at a shop?

The 4x6 inch (10x15 cm) paper size is the worldwide standard for photo printing machines at studios and cyber cafes. It holds 6 to 8 photos and typically costs only ₹5 to ₹10.
