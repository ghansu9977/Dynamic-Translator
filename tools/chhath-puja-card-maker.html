Chhath Puja Festival Significance & Custom Greeting Card Maker

Chhath Puja is one of the ancient Vedic festivals celebrated primarily in Bihar, Uttar Pradesh, Jharkhand, the Terai region of Nepal, and across Indian diaspora communities worldwide. Dedicated to Lord Surya (the Sun God) and Chhathi Maiya (Usha, the Goddess of Dawn), Chhath Puja represents supreme gratitude towards nature, the solar energy that sustains life, and spiritual self-purification.

During the four-day rigorous observance — beginning with Nahay-Khay, followed by Kharna, Sandhya Arghya (evening offering to the setting sun), and concluding with Usha Arghya (morning offering to the rising sun) — devotees (Vratis) observe strict 36-hour waterless fasts (Nirjala) and perform ritual prayers along riverbanks and water reservoirs.
