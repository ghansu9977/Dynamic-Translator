Sarkari Exam Photo & Signature Size Guidelines 2026 (SSC, UPSC, IBPS, Railway)

By RozgarTools Editorial Team • Updated for 2026 Exam Notifications • 7 min read

Every year, hundreds of thousands of competitive exam candidates face sudden application rejection simply due to non-compliant photograph uploads, blurry signatures, or incorrect file compression. Whether you are appearing for Staff Selection Commission (SSC) examinations, Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) Civil Services, Institute of Banking Personnel Selection (IBPS) PO/Clerk recruitments, or Railway Recruitment Boards (RRB), complying strictly with photo and signature specifications is your first critical step.

In this comprehensive guide, our technical team breaks down official recruitment dimensions, aspect ratios, file boundaries, and background standards, alongside common pitfalls that cause automated application portals to disqualify submissions.

Summary of Official Photo & Signature Requirements

Key Rules for Photographs (Do's and Don'ts)

  • Plain White Background: Always ensure the background is solid white or light neutral grey. Avoid textured curtains, brick walls, outdoor foliage, or shadows.
  • Face Coverage: Your face, ears, and neck must occupy between 70% and 80% of the entire frame. Avoid standing too far from the camera lens.
  • Neutral Facial Expression: Keep both eyes open, looking directly into the camera lens with your mouth closed. Do not smile broadly or tilt your head.
  • No Headgear or Dark Glasses: Hats, caps, hoods, and tinted sunglasses are strictly prohibited unless worn for certified religious obligations (even in which case the full face from forehead to chin must remain unobstructed).
  • Recent Capture: The photo must have been taken within the last 3 months prior to the application cut-off date to ensure accurate identity verification at the examination hall.

Signature Upload Standards

Exam portals utilize automated image validation to verify signature contrast and legibility:

  1. Sign on unruled, spotless white A4 or bond paper using a clean black or dark blue ink pen (gel or ballpoint). Never sign on lined notebook paper.
  2. Do not sign entirely in BLOCK or CAPITAL letters. Portals treat all-caps signatures as invalid and will flag the application during scrutiny.
  3. Crop tightly around the signature boundary before compressing to ensure the letters remain clear and sharp within the 10 KB to 20 KB threshold.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. What is the standard photo size for SSC CGL and CHSL online forms?

For SSC forms, the passport photo must be between 20 KB and 50 KB, in JPEG/JPG format, with dimensions of 3.5 cm width by 4.5 cm height (approximately 100 x 120 pixels). The background must be plain white or very light grey.

2. Why do competitive exam portals reject uploaded signatures?

Signatures are most frequently rejected because candidates sign in capital letters, use faint blue ink on lined notebook paper, or the file size exceeds the strict 10 KB to 20 KB boundary. Always sign in running handwriting with black or dark blue ink on clean unruled white paper.

3. Is name and date mandatory on photos for government recruitment in 2026?

Certain recruitment bodies (such as State Police and specific public service commissions) mandate that the candidate's name and the date of taking the photograph (DOPO) be printed clearly at the bottom. Check your specific official notification before applying.
