Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 30, 2026

🔒 Zero Server Storage Guarantee:

"We do not store or upload user images, documents, or personal data on our servers. All image compression, resizing, and calculations are executed 100% locally in the user's web browser using HTML5 Canvas API and modern browser technologies."

At RozgarTools ("we", "our", or "our website"), accessible from rozgartools.vercel.app, the privacy of our visitors is of paramount importance. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information that is collected and recorded and how we utilize it.

1. Image and Document Confidentiality

When you utilize our utilities (such as the Photo & Signature Resizer or Image to PDF Converter), your uploaded photos, certificates, or documents are never transmitted over the internet to remote servers.

  • All compression and resizing computations take place directly in your device's browser memory.
  • Once you close or refresh the browser tab, all temporary data is immediately cleared from memory.
  • We retain zero copies, metadata, or image caches.

2. Cookies and Local Storage

RozgarTools does not use tracking cookies to personally identify visitors. We solely utilize client-side localStorage to store user interface preferences, such as your selected visual theme (Dark Mode / Light Mode).

3. Advertising Disclosures and Third-Party Vendors

Third-party vendors, including Google, may serve advertisements on our website.

  • Google uses cookies (such as the DART cookie) to serve ads to users based on prior visits to our site and other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting the Google Ads Privacy Policy.

4. Web Server Log Files

Standard hosting providers (e.g. Vercel, Cloudflare) follow automated logging procedures. These logs record non-personally identifiable technical information such as internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, referral/exit pages, and date/time stamps strictly for system diagnostics and site reliability.

5. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)

We do not knowingly collect or solicit personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. Our utilities are designed for general academic, professional, and everyday utility use.

6. User Consent

By using our website, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its terms.

7. Contact Information

If you have questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, please contact us via our Contact Us page.
